## Name

Pebble for WhatsApp

## Short description (132 characters max)

Blur private content on WhatsApp Web. Peek at one item when you need it, or
cover the whole page with one shortcut.

## Category

Accessibility (secondary: Productivity)

## Language

English (United States)

## Long description

Pebble blurs the private parts of WhatsApp Web so the screen is safe to leave
open. Blur messages, photos, names, link previews, documents, the message
input and more, all at one strength, with per-group control when you need it.

To read one message, peek. Hover, click, or press and hold, and the blur
lifts for that item only, then comes right back. Chat videos pause while they
are blurred and only play when you reveal them.

To clear the whole screen, cover it. One button, or one shortcut (Alt+X by
default), drops a frosted layer over the page. Cover can also engage when the
page loads or after you go idle, and stays on until you release it.

Pebble also lives on the page as a small coral helper. Click it to reveal
everything without touching your settings. Hold it to peek. Slide onto a
timer and let everything blur itself again when the time runs out.

Highlights:

- One strength for the whole page, or per-group control with 27 content types
- Hover, Click and Hold peek modes, with an adjustable hover delay
- Auto-hide: a revealed item blurs itself again after a set time
- Whole-page cover with shortcut, on-load and inactivity triggers
- Light and dark themes, matching your system or fixed
- Keyboard support for tabs, radio groups and shortcuts
- Settings backup: copy or download as JSON, paste or file to restore
- No telemetry, no remote code, no accounts. Everything stays on your device
