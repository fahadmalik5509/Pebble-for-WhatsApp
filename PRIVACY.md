# Privacy Policy

Pebble for WhatsApp is a visual privacy tool. It blurs content on WhatsApp Web
in your own browser. It never sends your data anywhere.

## What the extension does

- It applies CSS blur to parts of the WhatsApp Web page you choose: messages,
  photos, names, previews and more.
- It can cover the whole page behind a frosted layer, on demand, on page load,
  or after inactivity.
- It shows a small on-page helper that you can click, hold or drag.

## What the extension does not do

- No telemetry or tracking.
- No remote code. No external fonts, images, or requests to any server.
- It does not read, copy or transmit your messages. The extension only applies
  CSS styles.
- No accounts, no sign-in, no ads.

## What is stored, and where

| Data | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Your settings (strength, groups, shortcuts, timers) | Browser extension storage, on this device |
| Cover state | Local extension storage, on this device |
| Helper position | Local extension storage, on this device |

Settings backups are created only when you choose Copy or Download in the
Backup section. The file goes to your clipboard or your downloads folder.
Nothing is uploaded.

If your browser profile syncs extension settings through your browser
account, your settings follow that browser's own sync. The extension itself
has no server.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| `storage` | Save your settings on your device |
| `scripting` | Apply the blur styles to the WhatsApp Web page |
| `https://web.whatsapp.com/*` | The only site the extension runs on |
